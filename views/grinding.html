Other commands (reference)

  • add — registers a channel. Not needed here: picking a channel above already means it's registered.
  • growth — checks a channel's current growth rate. Not wired up on the website yet (the backend route it reads from isn't open to other sites yet).
  • subcount — checks a channel's subscriber count. Already shown above once you pick a channel.

Latest updates (1.2)

  • Grinding method — shows whether a channel grinds from chat or the website. Switching is turned off for now: chat commands and the buttons both work.
  • More commands — Short, Music Video, Phonk, Song, Meme, Moai and Viral (Viral has its own 5-minute cooldown, like Boost).
  • Real amounts — each button now shows the most it can give in Season 10 (commands give a random amount up to half the old number).
  • New look — a cleaner design that follows your device's light or dark mode, and loads much faster.
  • Live search — the channel list filters as you type.
  • Grind time limit — each channel gets 12 hours of grinding in any 24 hours. Time left shows under the subscriber count.
  • Shared cooldown — sending a command locks all the command buttons until its cooldown ends. Boost keeps its own 5-minute cooldown.
  • Quick checks — a short math question now and then while grinding, to stop macros.
  • Safer claiming — claiming or re-claiming a channel now needs proof you own it, through live chat or Google.
